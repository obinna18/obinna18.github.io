Mobile · Web · Fintech-Adjacent
Cionti
The Problem

LinkedIn is too formal. Google Maps misses the mechanic on your street. Skilled workers in Nigerian informal markets have no digital presence, and people looking for them have no reliable way to find them.

The Build

A hyperlocal skill discovery platform targeting Effurun, Warri, and Delta State. Users create skill profiles, get discovered on a map, build trust through referrals and ratings, and connect via real-time Firestore chat.

Trust Score formula: 0.5×referrals + 0.3×rating + 0.2×verification.

Outcome

Web prototype live on GitHub Pages. Full Android (Kotlin/MVVM) build in progress. Full launch targeted June 2027. Profitable by 2030.

QUEVA
The Problem

Students spend hours manually creating study questions from lecture PDFs. No tool was built specifically for Nigerian university students combining PDF parsing with an AI study assistant.

The Build

AI study companion at obinna18.github.io/QUEVA. Upload any PDF, ask questions about it, generate quizzes, export results. Powered by Groq LLaMA 3.3-70B via a Vercel proxy, with Firebase Auth for user persistence.

DLCF FUPRE · Academic Unit Designer
DLCF Design Work
The Brief

DLCF FUPRE needed consistent, well-designed social media content to keep students engaged academically, especially during high-pressure exam seasons. The audience: undergraduates who needed motivation without feeling lectured.

Problem & Approach

Problem: Generic motivational posts get scrolled past. Students needed content that felt real, specific to campus life, and visually sharp enough to share. Approach: Built a recurring content system: a motivation campaign, a practical checklist series, a conversational FAQ format, and a quote series. Each with a distinct visual identity that still felt like one brand.

Pieces & Solutions

Stay Grounded Campaign: Bold typographic design with orange/navy identity, built for Instagram shareability during exam pressure periods.

Before-Class Checklist: Tilted notepad motif infographic, part of the recurring #Dailyacademictips series to encourage student preparation.

Academic FAQ Series: Conversational Q&A with yellow pill labels: non-preachy, answering real objections students use to justify skipping class.

Quote Series (Vol. 1 & 2): Frosted glass cards on study-desk backgrounds with keyword highlights. Instantly identifiable in a student's feed.

DSN FUPRE Design Work
The Brief

As Chapter Lead of the Data Scientists Network at FUPRE, I needed to design visuals that would attract new members, communicate what DSN does, and turn passive students into active community members, especially during semester breaks when engagement typically drops.

Result
